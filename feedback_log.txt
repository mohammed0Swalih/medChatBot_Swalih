Diabetes (diabetes mellitus) is a condition in which the body either does not produce enough insulin or produces insulin that does not work properly. As a result, blood glucose (sugar) stays high—a state called hyperglycemia. Over time, untreated diabetes can damage the eyes, kidneys, nerves, heart, blood vessels, and other organs. It is typically classified into type 1 (formerly called juvenile‑onset or insulin‑dependent) and type 2 (formerly called adult‑onset or non‑insulin‑dependent) diabetes.
